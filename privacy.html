Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page explains how BudgetBase handles information when you visit this website.

Information we collect directly

BudgetBase does not have user accounts, a checkout or a contact form, and we do not ask you for personal information such as your name, address, email or payment details.

Analytics

Analytics only runs if you choose Accept in our cookie banner. If you choose Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and sets no cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

When it is on, we use Google Analytics to understand how people use this website. It may collect information such as the pages you view, the links you click, the website or app you came from (for example Pinterest), your approximate location, your device and browser type, and your IP address. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers for this purpose.

We use this information only to see which pages and categories are popular and to improve the website. We do not use it to identify you personally. You can learn how Google handles this data in Google's explanation of how it uses data from sites that use its services, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Pinterest

We share our products on Pinterest, and you may arrive here from a pin. This website contains a Pinterest verification tag that only confirms we own the website. It does not collect information about visitors. Pinterest has its own privacy policy for what it collects on its own platform.

Other third-party services

This website relies on other services that may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type or device when your browser loads their content:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts provides the font used on the website.
  • Google Sheets, through a third-party data service, supplies the product list.
  • Temu hosts the product images and, when you click a product link, the page you land on.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy, and we do not control how they use data.

Affiliate links and cookies

Our product links are affiliate links. When you click one, you are taken to Temu, which may place cookies or similar technologies on your device so that a purchase can be linked to BudgetBase and we can earn a commission. We do not see your Temu account, your orders or your payment information. Please read Temu's own privacy policy to learn how it handles your data.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some features of the services above may not work as expected, but you can still browse this website.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions

For help with an order, see our Contact page. To learn more about this website, see our About page.
