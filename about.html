About BudgetBase

BudgetBase is a small, independent website that gathers useful products found on Temu, such as car accessories, tech gadgets and home essentials, in one place so they are easier to browse and search.

What we do, and what we don't

  • We do not sell, store or ship any product.
  • When you click View Deal on Temu, you leave BudgetBase and go to Temu's website, where you complete your purchase.
  • Payments, delivery, returns and refunds are handled entirely by Temu.

How we choose products

We browse Temu and pick items that look useful, popular or good value. We have not necessarily tested every product, and the names and images come from the Temu listings. Please read the full description, reviews and seller information on Temu before you buy.

Affiliate disclosure

BudgetBase participates in Temu's affiliate program. If you click a link on this website and make a purchase, we may earn a commission. This comes at no extra cost to you and does not change the price you pay. Commissions help us keep the website running. Our product links are marked as sponsored.

Prices and availability

We do not show prices because they change often on Temu. The final price, stock and delivery details are always the ones shown on Temu at the time you buy.

Questions?

For help with an order, please read our Contact page. To learn how we handle data, see our Privacy page.
